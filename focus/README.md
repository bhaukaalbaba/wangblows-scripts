**Solution:**

Because the geniuses who birthed this abomination neglected to include even rudimentary cleanup functionality, I, in my infinite wisdom (and frankly, infinite patience considering the source), had to craft a rescue script. Think of it as a high-tech, highly caffeinated babysitter. ☕🛡️

**Script details:**

Welcome to the ultimate digital exorcism script, a.k.a. the "IIS Cleanup and Performance Resurrection Tool." This bad boy is designed to do everything short of making your server fall in love with you. From purging the digital junk hiding in your temp folders to summoning admin privileges, this script has your back.

**Script Features**

1. **Date & Time Wizardry**: 🧙‍♂️ Grabs the current date and time, pads those single-digit months and days, and creates an epic log file with a name that even a cyberpunk hero would envy.
2. **Admin Privileges Check**: 🛡️ Because, let's be honest, without admin powers, you’re just a peasant in the kingdom of IT. If the script isn’t running with the magic powers it needs, it will promptly nag you to do so.
3. **IIS Service Stop**: ✋ Halts the mighty IIS service, so we can clean up the mess without any pesky interruptions. Failure is not an option, but if it happens, the script will bow out gracefully.
4. **Temp File De-Crapification**: 🗑️ Targets those notorious temp directories and declares war on their contents. Any file standing in its way will be deleted with extreme prejudice, unless it’s stubborn—then it just gets a pass.
5. **IIS Service Start**: 🚀 Breathes life back into the IIS service, ensuring your web apps rise from the ashes like a digital phoenix.
6. **IIS Service Status Check**: 🔍 Inspects the status of your IIS service on ports 80 and 443, because we're thorough like that. Logs the results for your geeky pleasure.
7. **Logging Everything**: 📝 Keeps an annotated log of every step taken and decision made, ensuring you can relive the magic of this script's operation whenever you desire.

**Usage**

1. **Run As Administrator**: 👑 Remember, this script requires admin privileges. So, right-click and choose "Run as Administrator," or suffer the script’s wrath.
2. **Watch the Magic Unfold**: 🌟 Sit back, relax, and watch as this script performs its digital exorcism and de-crapification routine.
3. **Check the Logs**: 📜 Dive into the log file to see the step-by-step documentation of your server’s revival.
